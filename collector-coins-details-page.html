Best Price principle

We are particularly interested in purchasing gold coins and are paying high prices for them, due to the record high gold prices in 2024. We purchase your coins according to the "best price" principle; you will receive either the collector's purchase value or the gold purchase value, whichever is higher. Our decades of experience in both coin and precious metal trading guarantees a completely reliable transaction

Silver coins and medals

Due to the current high price of silver, we purchase all types of silver coins: bullion coins, circulation coins, commemorative coins, etc. We also purchase medals and themed series made of gold and silver. These are coins without face value, privately produced by coin mail-order companies.
