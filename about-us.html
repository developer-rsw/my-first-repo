A business with a heart and a history

For more than two decades, we’ve been known for honest, fair trade in coins, precious metals, and jewelry. As an owner-operated business, we value trust, personal consultation, and long-term relationships with our clients.

Over 25 years of experience

Based in the heart of Kiel

Personalized and discreet advice

Fair market-based pricing

Our Roots – Our Values

1980s

Started trading at Beselerallee 70, focused solely on stamps and coins.

1990s

Introduced precious metals trading alongside coins and stamps.

1992s

Became a certified member of the German Coin Dealers’ Association, ensuring fair and professional transactions.

1998s

Expanded into wholesale trading, supplying banks and savings banks with coins and precious metals.

2000s–Today

Built strong nationwide dealer connections, allowing us to offer top market prices for purchases.

Gold and silver as investments

For over 20 years, you have been able to purchase gold and silver as an investment from us. We always have gold bars, gold, and silver coins in stock for you to take home – for discreet and anonymous cash payment (up to €1,999.99). We source our precious metals directly from reputable refineries or in sealed original containers from state mints. This offers you the greatest possible assurance regarding the quality of the bars and coins we sell.

We also always have gold and silver bars, Krugerrands, and silver bullion coins such as the Vienna Philharmonic in stock for immediate delivery.

We would be delighted if you would visit us in person to experience our service and expertise for yourself, and we look forward to hearing from you!

Job opening: Ambitious talents wanted – Grow together in a great team!

We're looking for dedicated people who want to grow with us and work in an environment they feel comfortable in. Exciting tasks and a team that supports you every step of the way await you.

Your tasks:

  • You will provide comprehensive advice to our customers on all aspects of buying and selling precious metals, jewelry, capital goods, and diamonds, both for private and commercial clients.
  • You will analyze and evaluate precious metals, jewelry, and diamonds with an expert eye.
  • Your responsibilities will also include independent cash management (cash and debit card payments), including daily closing.
  • You will record and post goods movements and prepare the necessary receipts for our customers.

What you bring with you:

  • A completed apprenticeship as a bank clerk or a comparable commercial qualification. Career changers with relevant experience, for example, from sales in upscale retail or the high-price segment, are also welcome.
  • Experience in cash management and knowledge of physical precious metal investments. A customer-focused and sales-oriented attitude, and ideally experience in counter sales, round out your profile.
  • Excellent written and spoken German skills.
  • You are a team player, work independently and in a structured manner, and are characterized by a solution-oriented approach, even in complex situations.

What we offer:

  • Above-average pay that recognizes and rewards your performance.
  • The option of a four-day work week, which offers you flexibility and a healthy work-life balance.
  • Flexible working hours for optimal work-life balance.
  • If you are interested in becoming part of a dynamic and growing team and developing yourself in an exciting environment, we look forward to receiving your application!
